A numbers-led front page for 25 November 2026: 50 stories across 10 regions.

**Hero stories**

1. **Trump hits 14 partners with new tariffs, China at 20%.** Higher import costs will ripple through global supply chains and could push consumer prices up around the world.
2. **AI chip selloff sinks Kospi 6.16% to 4,697.51.** The selloff wipes out weeks of AI-chip gains and signals investor doubt about sky-high chip valuations. Samsung fell 9.87% and SK Hynix 11.05%.
3. **Shenzhou-24 carries the first foreign astronaut to Tiangong.** China opens its space station to international partners, marking a new era of crewed space diplomacy. Muhammad Zeeshan Ali of Pakistan joins the crew.

**Also inside:** the Bogor gold mine collapse (9 killed, 17 trapped), tariff fallout, chip supply chains, COP31 in Antalya, and 47 numbered stories across the US, China, Korea, Indonesia, Europe, the Middle East, space, markets, tech and climate.

## How to run

```sh
python3 gen.py        # renders entry.png (build time, Pillow)
python3 main.py       # copies entry.png to MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (stdlib only)
```
